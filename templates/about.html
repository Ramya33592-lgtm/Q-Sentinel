Mathematical Formulation
Quantum Physics
1. Angle Feature Encoding:

Continuous network features $x \in [0, \pi]^n$ are mapped to single-qubit rotations:

|ψ_0(x)⟩ = ⨂_{i=0}^{n-1} R_z(2x_i) R_y(x_i) H |0⟩
2. Circular Entanglement (CNOT Ring):

Creates Bell-like non-local state correlations between packet length, flow duration, and protocol velocity:

U_{ent} = ∏_{i=0}^{n-1} CX(q_i, q_{(i+1) \pmod n})
3. Variational Ansatz & Expectation Value:

Parameterized rotations $W(\theta)$ optimize classification boundary. The attack probability is derived via Pauli-Z expectation on the primary register:

⟨Z_0⟩ = ⟨0| (U_Φ W(θ))† Z_0 (U_Φ W(θ)) |0⟩,   P(Attack) = (1 - ⟨Z_0⟩)/2
Why Quantum in Cybersecurity?
Innovation
Exponential Hilbert Space Capacity:
$n$ qubits simulate an expressive state space of $2^n$ dimensions, allowing a compact 4-qubit register to detect non-linear attack interactions without massive neural networks.
Real Execution via Local Simulator:
Q-Sentinel uses Qiskit 2.5 with full local Statevector computation, enabling zero-cloud, privacy-compliant on-premise execution for classified SOC environments.
Hybrid Defense System:
Leverages the ultra-fast inference speed of Random Forests alongside the geometric sensitivity of Variational Quantum circuits for defense-in-depth.
